What does "schema" actually do?

Schema is structured data that tells search engines and AI tools what a page is, what your business does, and why you're a credible source. Most WordPress sites have a bit of it, auto-generated by Yoast or Rank Math. That's typically just top-level Article and Breadcrumb markup. It doesn't describe your services, your offerings, your FAQs, or the specific signals AI answer engines look at. That's the layer this service builds.

Which platforms does this work on?

It's built for WordPress. The schema, the styling, and the articles all arrive ready to drop straight in: clean HTML, a snippet of CSS, and the schema for the page header. Uploading takes a few minutes per article.

Other platforms work too. Webflow, Ghost, Shopify, custom builds: the articles and their schema slot in fine, though the upload steps vary by platform.

Wix and Squarespace are the harder ones. They make it more awkward to add the kind of structured data this service relies on, so the foundation month doesn't land as neatly. It can still work, but it's worth a conversation first.
