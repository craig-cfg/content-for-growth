What does "schema" actually do?

Schema is structured data that tells search engines and AI tools what a page is, what your business does, and why you're a credible source. Most WordPress sites have a bit of it, auto-generated by Yoast or Rank Math — but that's typically just top-level Article and Breadcrumb markup. It doesn't describe your services, your offerings, your FAQs, or the specific signals AI answer engines look at. That's the layer this service builds.
